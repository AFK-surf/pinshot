一个快捷键，截图、标注、贴图一气呵成 One shortcut to capture, mark up and pin

按下 Ctrl+Alt+A，屏幕即刻定格；画框、加箭头、打马赛克，复制或贴在屏幕一角，随手对照。 Press Ctrl+Alt+A and the screen freezes at once: box it, point at it, blur it, then copy it or pin it to a corner of the screen.

PinShot 是一款 Windows 截图工具，体积不到 5 MB，常驻托盘、开销很小。截图几乎不用等待：它一直保留着屏幕的最新画面，按键后约五十毫秒遮罩就出现。HDR 显示器上截出来的颜色也和眼睛看到的一致。

点一下选中窗口，拖动框选区域；矩形、椭圆、箭头、画笔、马赛克、序号、文字一应俱全，画完的标记还能再选中修改。截好的图可以钉在屏幕上：拖动、缩放、调透明度，双击即关。

PinShot is a screenshot tool for Windows, under 5 MB, that sits in the tray and costs little while it waits. Capturing is all but instant: it keeps the screen's latest picture ready, and the overlay appears about fifty milliseconds after the key. On HDR monitors the colours come out as you see them.

Click to take a window, drag for an area; rectangle, ellipse, arrow, pen, mosaic, numbered markers and text are all there, and every mark can be selected and changed afterwards. A capture can be pinned on top of everything: drag it, zoom it, fade it, double-click to close.

定格即截Frozen on the key

按键那一刻的画面就是截到的画面：菜单、悬停提示都不会因为截图而消失。显示器睡醒、解锁之后也照样是当下的画面。

What was on screen as you pressed the key is what you get: menus and tooltips stay put. After the monitor sleeps or the PC unlocks, it is still the screen as it is now.

标记可再改Marks stay editable

每画完一个标记就自动选中，马上能改颜色和粗细、拖动、删除；马赛克也一样。撤销、重做随时可用。

Each mark is selected as soon as it is drawn: change its colour or width, move it, delete it, mosaics included. Undo and redo all the way.

钉在屏幕上Pinned on top

贴图浮在所有窗口之上，适合对照资料、临摹界面。滚轮缩放，Ctrl+滚轮调透明度，右键可复制或另存。

Pins float over every window, for reference while you work. Wheel to zoom, Ctrl+wheel for opacity, right-click to copy or save.

轻巧省心Light and quiet

单个程序文件，不联网、不收集任何数据。设置里可改快捷键、保存位置、开机自启和快门声。

One program file; it never goes online and collects nothing. Shortcuts, save folder, start with Windows and the shutter sound are in the settings.
